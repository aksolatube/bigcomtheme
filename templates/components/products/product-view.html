Installation guide ☀

Choosing Your Roof Covering

Choose the option that matches the material covering the outside of your roof where the Solatube will be installed.

Flat Roof

Select this for a flat or nearly flat roof finished with a continuous membrane such as EPDM, bitumen, or PVC.

Profiled Tile Roof

Select this for a sloped roof covered with overlapping clay or concrete tiles that create a raised or wavy profile.

Important: Look at the roof covering at the planned installation point. Roof pitch alone does not determine this choice. If you are unsure, confirm the material with your installer before ordering.
{{> components/common/modal/modal-close-btn }}
Installation guide ☀

Choosing Your Roof Tile Type

Choose the option that matches the shape of the tiles or slate at the planned installation point.

High Profile Tile

Select this for pantiles, S-shaped tiles, or other raised and wavy clay or concrete tiles with noticeable ridges and troughs.

Flat Tile or Slate

Select this for flat interlocking tiles, plain tiles, or natural slate that creates a relatively even roof surface.

Important: Choose by the shape of the roof covering, not the roof pitch. “Flat Tile or Slate” does not mean a flat membrane roof. If your roof uses EPDM, bitumen, PVC, or another membrane, confirm the correct flashing with your installer before ordering.
